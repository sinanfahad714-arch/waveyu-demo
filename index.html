About
Team Waveyu

We joke that none of us planned to end up here. Kaspars came to 'find himself,' Noah was escaping burnout, Marcus was running from a bad breakup. Sofia and Kristina came for a month and never left. We all just... stayed. We met randomly – at surf breaks, beach bars, one at a full moon party. Started hanging out, helping each other with gigs here and there. Eventually we realized we worked well together and had the same crazy idea: why not start a surf camp? That was three years ago.

Now we're on our 20th camp, and we just want you to experience the same thing that made us all stay.

Steps

Make it happen in just four steps

We've made the entire process smooth and hassle-free. Our team guides you through every step from booking to arrival — just show up ready for waves.

01

Choose your package

We have three options: Neighbor (twin room with 2 single beds), Double (king bed for couples or solo travelers), and Squad (group suite for friends). Pick what fits your budget and travel style.

02

Leave your contact details

Fill out a quick form with your name, email, surf level, and preferred package. Our manager will reach out within 24 hours to answer all your questions, confirm availability, and guide you through the next steps.

03

Complete payment

Once everything is confirmed, secure your spot with payment. You'll receive a welcome pack with all camp details and a packing list, and be added to your camp WhatsApp group to meet everyone before departure.

04

Get ready to go

We'll send arrival instructions and arrange airport pickup. Just bring yourself and good vibes — we'll take care of everything else. See you in Canggu!

faq

Frequently
Asked Questions

01

Do I need to know how to surf already?

Not at all! Most people who come are complete beginners or have only surfed a few times. Our instructors work with all levels, and we split into groups so you're learning with people at your pace. If you can swim and are willing to try, you're ready.

02

I'm traveling alone — will I feel out of place?

Actually, most people come solo. That's the beauty of it — everyone's in the same boat (or on the same board). By day two, it feels like you've known each other forever. Solo travelers often tell us this was the best decision they made.

03

What's not included in the price?

Lunches (we usually grab these together at local spots, budget $5–12), personal expenses like extra massages or shopping, travel insurance, and your flight to Bali. Everything else — accommodation, breakfast, dinner, surf lessons, activities, airport transfers — is covered.

04

What if the dates don't work for me?

We run multiple sessions throughout the year. If these dates don't fit your schedule, drop us a message and we'll let you know when the next camps are happening. You can also join our email list to be the first to know about new dates.

05

Is Bali safe for travelers?

Yes! Bali, especially Canggu, is very safe and welcoming to international travelers. The local community is used to surfers and tourists, and we'll be with you for most activities. We'll also give you a full safety briefing on day one covering everything from scooter riding to ocean awareness.

06

Can I extend my stay at the villa?

Sometimes! If the villa isn't booked for another camp immediately after, we can often arrange for you to stay a few extra nights at a discounted rate. Just let us know in advance and we'll check availability. Many people do this to explore Bali on their own after the camp ends.
